# 2026-11-10 · Cycle 01 · Stay with her

**Scope:** CLOUD, era 08, “A small way home.” This is a simulated design review. Its scores are provisional and apply to this chapter, not the full game.

## Conversation

**A · Narrative director:** The old sequence calls the last stone “home” as soon as it gets wet. The girl is still on the trail when the world can advance. The important event is her arrival.

**B · Gameplay designer:** Make rain a direction she hears. Then the player flies alongside her. Keep the first stone short so the rules become clear before the crossing.

**C · VR and accessibility designer:** Let distance and existing mist decide when she stops. Give the player a way back from either condition. Keep the rule in the simulation; the keyboard version should not need a mouse to aim.

**D · QA and player advocate:** Don't spend water while the objective is walking together. The marker must point to the girl, and the next stone must wait until she reaches the current one. Test the whiteout while the crossing is unfinished.

**A:** Give her a line at the porch that makes the later yellow scarf matter: “If you see yellow again, will you know it's me?”

**B:** Require one deliberate goodbye with F. That makes the next era something the player chooses after seeing her safe.

**C:** Keep WASD for translation and use arrow keys for the view. Rotate and translate separately so looking alone does not disperse the mist.

**D:** The extra travel could feel slow. Keep the crossing and distance rules reversible. Judge the timing in Unity before expanding this pattern elsewhere.

## Implemented in the Unity project

- Rain signals one stop at a time. Each leg uses the same route in simulation and rendering. The girl pauses if the cloud drifts out of range, flies too high, or disappears in the mist.
- Direction marker and context text follow the girl. A short F hold at the porch makes the chapter ready to pass through time. Her goodbye sets up the later reunion.
- WASD moves; arrow keys turn and tilt the camera; Enter starts. Mouse and the existing action keys remain available.
- Changed: CloudStory.cs, CloudJourney.cs, CloudWorld.cs, CloudHud.cs, CloudFlight.cs, CloudDirector.cs, CloudVerify.cs, the offline regression runner, and instructions. The existing art and audio stay as they were.

It checked the escort distance and altitude, mist hysteresis, recovery after whiteout, arrival at the actual waypoint, the reserved water, and the farewell gate. The project archive and asset references passed validation. Unity Play Mode, keyboard feel, subtitle timing, and VR still need device checks.
