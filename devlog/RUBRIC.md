# CLOUD · Review rubric

Score the same chapter or scenario before and after each change. State what was exercised, what is an estimate, and what still needs a playtest. A simulated discussion is design evidence, not a user study.

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Narrative causality | 25 | The player sees why an action changes what happens next. Setup and payoff survive the transition into the following era. |
| Play and tension | 25 | The scenario has a clear goal, meaningful pressure and a readable consequence, with no needless repetition or opaque fail state. |
| Keyboard clarity | 20 | A new player can aim, move, perform the story action and recover with keyboard alone. Prompts match the action they can perform. |
| Correctness and recovery | 20 | Focused regression tests and a Unity run verify success, interruption and recovery, including entering from a chapter checkpoint. |
| Path to VR | 10 | Story decisions stay independent of key bindings. The eventual headset run verifies interaction readability, comfort and recovery. |

100 is a target. Preserve deductions when Unity, player, or headset evidence is missing. Prefer a small finished change to three speculative mechanics. Revisit older scores when a playtest contradicts the assumptions.
